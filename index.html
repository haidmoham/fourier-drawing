from the picture to the equation +
z(t) = c₀ + ∑ cₖ ei2πkt

Your drawing becomes a sequence of points, written as z = x + iy. A discrete Fourier transform finds their rotating components. Each coefficient cₖ sets a circle’s radius and starting angle; k sets its direction and revolutions per loop. We add the largest components first.

The drawing is sampled at 256 evenly spaced positions around a closed path. If your stroke is open, the dashed seam joins its ends. The reconstruction repeats that complete loop. These circles are an exact representation of the computed sum, not an illustration layered over it.
